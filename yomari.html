Yomari

A traditional Newari sweet made with rice flour and filled with delicious chaku or jaggery.

Back to Home

Cooking time: 40 mins

Ingredients

  • Rice flour - 2 cups
  • Warm water - as required
  • Chaku or jaggery - 1 cup, chopped
  • Sesame seeds - 2 tbsp
  • Grated coconut - 2 tbsp, optional
  • Ghee - 1 tsp, optional
  • Water - 2 tbsp, for preparing the filling if needed

Cooking Procedure

  1. Dry-roast the sesame seeds in a pan over low heat until fragrant.
  2. In a small pan, combine the chopped chaku or jaggery with a little water and heat gently until it melts.
  3. Add the roasted sesame seeds and grated coconut to the melted jaggery. Mix well and allow the filling to cool slightly.
  4. Place the rice flour in a bowl and gradually add warm water, mixing until a soft, smooth dough forms.
  5. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for about 10 minutes.
  6. Take a small portion of dough and roll it into a ball.
  7. Use your fingers to shape the dough into a hollow, elongated shell with a pointed top.
  8. Fill the hollow with the prepared chaku mixture.
  9. Carefully pinch and shape the top to seal the filling while maintaining the traditional yomari shape.
  10. Repeat the process with the remaining dough and filling.
  11. Arrange the yomari in a steamer, leaving a little space between each one.
  12. Steam for about 15-20 minutes until the rice flour dough is cooked and becomes firm.
  13. Carefully remove the yomari from the steamer and let them cool slightly before serving.

Serving

Serve warm as a traditional Newari sweet, especially during Yomari Punhi and festive occasions.

Enjoy your homemade Yomari!
