Carrot Pudding

Sweet, creamy, and delicious carrot pudding made with milk, sugar, and nuts.

Back to Home

Cooking time:40 mins

Ingredients

  • Carrots - 500 g
  • Full-fat milk - 500 ml
  • Sugar - 1/2 cup, or to taste
  • Ghee - 3 tbsp
  • Cardamom powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Cashews - 2 tbsp, chopped
  • Raisins - 2 tbsp
  • Almonds - 2 tbsp, chopped, optional

Cooking Procedure

  1. Wash, peel, and grate the carrots.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of ghee in a large pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the grated carrots and sauté for about 5 minutes until they soften slightly.
  4. Pour in the milk and mix well.
  5. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until the carrots become soft and most of the milk evaporates. This may take 25–35 minutes.
  6. Add the sugar and stir well. The mixture may become thinner as the sugar dissolves.
  7. Continue cooking for another 5–10 minutes until the mixture thickens.
  8. Heat the remaining ghee in a small pan and lightly fry the cashews, raisins, and almonds.
  9. Add the fried nuts, raisins, and cardamom powder to the carrot mixture.
  10. Mix everything well and cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  11. Turn off the heat when the pudding becomes thick and creamy.

Serving

Serve warm or chilled, garnished with chopped nuts.

Enjoy your homemade Carrot Pudding!
