Chicken chilli

Crispy, spicy, and delicious homemade chicken.

Back to Home

Ingredients

For the Chicken

  • Boneless chicken breast or leg pieces - 1 kg

For the Batter

  • Refined flour - 2 tbsp
  • Cornflour - 2 tbsp
  • Salt to taste
  • White pepper powder - 1/2 tbsp
  • Water as required
  • Ginger-garlic paste - 1/2 tbsp
  • Hot sauce - 1/2 tbsp, or to taste
  • Lemon juice - 5–6 drops

For the Sauce

  • Tomato ketchup - 3–4 tbsp
  • Salt to taste
  • White pepper powder - 1/2 tbsp
  • Hot sauce - 1/2 tbsp
  • Soy sauce - 3–4 drops (optional)
  • MSG - 1/5 tbsp (optional)
  • Vinegar - a few drops
  • Spring onion or coriander - 1 handful
  • Sesame seeds - 1/3 tbsp, for garnish
  • Cooking oil - as required for frying

Cooking Procedure

  1. Cut the boneless chicken into small, bite-sized pieces.
  2. In a large bowl, add refined flour, cornflour, salt, white pepper powder, ginger-garlic paste, hot sauce, and lemon juice.
  3. Add water little by little and mix until you get a smooth, thick batter.
  4. Add the chicken pieces and mix thoroughly so every piece is coated with the batter.
  5. Cover the bowl and let the chicken marinate in the refrigerator for 20–30 minutes.
  6. Heat cooking oil in a deep pan. Carefully add the chicken pieces in small batches.
  7. Fry until golden and crispy, making sure the chicken is fully cooked. The thickest pieces should reach an internal temperature of 74°C (165°F).
  8. Remove the chicken from the oil and place it on a rack or paper towel to drain excess oil.
  9. For the sauce, heat a separate pan over low heat. Add tomato ketchup, salt, white pepper powder, hot sauce, optional soy sauce, optional MSG, and vinegar.
  10. Mix the sauce well. Add a little water if it is too thick, and heat gently until combined.
  11. Add the fried chicken to the sauce and toss until all the pieces are evenly coated.
  12. Turn off the heat and garnish with chopped spring onion or coriander and sesame seeds.

Serving

Enjoy your Nepali style chicken chilli!!!! with your favourite drink or side dish!
