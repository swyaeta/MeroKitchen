Pita Sandwich

30 min

A fresh, flavourful sandwich packed with vegetables and creamy sauce.

Ingredients

  • Pita bread - 2 pieces
  • Cucumber - 1/2 medium
  • Tomato - 1 medium
  • Onion - 1/2 medium
  • Lettuce - a few leaves
  • Paneer or cooked chicken - 150 g (optional)
  • Plain yogurt - 3 tbsp
  • Mayonnaise - 1 tbsp (optional)
  • Lemon juice - 1 tsp
  • Black pepper powder - 1/4 tsp
  • Salt - to taste
  • Olive oil - 1 tsp (optional)

Cooking Procedure

  1. Wash the cucumber, tomato, lettuce, and onion thoroughly.
  2. Cut the cucumber, tomato, and onion into thin slices. Tear the lettuce into smaller pieces.
  3. If using paneer, cut it into strips and lightly fry it in a pan with a little oil until golden. If using chicken, cook it thoroughly before slicing.
  4. In a small bowl, mix the yogurt, mayonnaise, lemon juice, black pepper, and salt to make the sauce.
  5. Warm the pita bread in a pan for about 1–2 minutes on each side until soft and slightly toasted.
  6. Carefully open each pita bread to form a pocket.
  7. Spread the prepared sauce inside the pita pocket.
  8. Add lettuce, cucumber, tomato, onion, and your cooked paneer or chicken.
  9. Add a little extra sauce if desired, then gently press the pita together.
  10. Serve fresh and enjoy!

Serving

Serve immediately as a quick breakfast, lunch, or snack. You can customise the fillings with your favourite vegetables and protein.
