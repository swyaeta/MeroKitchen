Rasbari

Soft, spongy, and juicy milk balls soaked in sweet sugar syrup.

Cooking time: 1 hrs

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Full-fat milk - 1 litre
  • Lemon juice - 2 tbsp
  • Water - 4 cups
  • Sugar - 1 cup
  • Cardamom pods - 2, optional
  • Ice-cold water - as required

Cooking Procedure

  1. Pour the milk into a pot and heat it until it comes to a boil.
  2. Reduce the heat and gradually add lemon juice while stirring gently until the milk separates into curds and whey.
  3. Turn off the heat and add some ice-cold water to stop the cooking process.
  4. Strain the curds through a clean muslin cloth and rinse them with cold water to remove the lemon flavour.
  5. Hang the cloth for about 30 minutes to drain the excess water.
  6. Transfer the chenna (fresh cheese) to a plate and knead it until smooth and soft.
  7. Divide the mixture into small portions and roll them into smooth balls without cracks.
  8. In a large pot, combine the water and sugar. Bring the mixture to a boil, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  9. Carefully add the chenna balls to the boiling syrup, leaving enough space for them to expand.
  10. Cover the pot and cook over medium heat for about 15-20 minutes, keeping the syrup boiling gently.
  11. Turn off the heat and allow the rasbari to cool in the syrup.
  12. Refrigerate until chilled for a refreshing dessert.

Serving

Serve chilled in a bowl with plenty of sweet syrup.

Enjoy your soft an juicyyyy homemade Rasbari!!!
