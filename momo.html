Momo

Homemade, delicious and full of flavour.

Nepali version of dumplings

Cooking time: 2hrs

Back to Home

Ingredients

For Kima (Filling)

  • Paneer – 300 g
  • Or meat or veg (According to the momo your making)
  • Onions – 2 medium-sized
  • Ginger-garlic paste – 1 tbsp
  • Coriander leaves – 1 handful
  • Boiled potato – 100 g
  • Butter – 1 tbsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • White pepper powder – 1 tbsp
  • Momo masala – 1 tbsp
  • Coriander and cumin powder – 1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce – a few drops
  • MSG – some (optional)
  • Roasted crushed peanuts – 1/2 handful
  • Chopped spring onions – 1/2 handful

For Dough

  • Flour – 500 g
  • Water – as needed

Cooking Procedure

Step 1: Prepare the Filling

  1. Crumble the paneer into a bowl.
  2. Finely chop the onions, coriander leaves and spring onions.
  3. Add the boiled potato and ginger-garlic paste.
  4. Add butter, salt, white pepper, momo masala, coriander and cumin powder.
  5. Add a few drops of soy sauce and MSG if desired.
  6. Add the roasted crushed peanuts and chopped spring onions.
  7. Mix everything together until evenly combined.

Step 2: Prepare the Dough

  1. Put the flour in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Add water gradually and knead until a firm, smooth dough forms.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rest for about 20 minutes.

Step 3: Shape the Momo

  1. Divide the dough into small portions.
  2. Roll each portion into a thin, round wrapper.
  3. Place a spoonful of filling in the centre.
  4. Fold and seal the edges to shape the momo.
  5. Repeat until all the filling and dough are used.

Step 4: Steam the Momo

  1. Grease the steamer tray lightly with oil.
  2. Arrange the momos with a little space between them.
  3. Steam over boiling water for approximately 10–15 minutes, until the wrappers are cooked and the filling is piping hot.

Step 5: Serve

Serve the hot momo with spicy tomato achar or your favourite dipping sauce.
