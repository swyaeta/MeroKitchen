French fries

Crispy fried potato and best with ketchup

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Potatoes-500g
  • Salt acc to taste
  • Cornflour-2 tbs (optional)
  • Black pepper powder- 1/2 tbs
  • Chilli powder - acc to taste
  • Cooking oil (ofc lol)- acc to the depth of your pan, jus enough to deep fry

Cooking Procedure

  1. Wash and peel the potatoes.
  2. Cut the potatoes into long, even strips so they cook evenly.
  3. Put the potato strips in a bowl of cold water and soak for 30 minutes to remove excess starch.
  4. Drain the water and dry the potato strips completely using a clean kitchen towel.
  5. Sprinkle cornflour over the potatoes, if using, and mix until they are lightly coated.
  6. Heat cooking oil in a deep pan over medium heat. Carefully fry the potatoes in small batches until they are tender but not yet golden.
  7. Remove the potatoes and let them rest for a few minutes.
  8. Increase the oil temperature slightly and fry the potatoes again in small batches until golden and crispy.
  9. Remove the fries and place them on a rack or paper towel to drain excess oil.
  10. Sprinkle salt, black pepper powder, or chilli powder according to your taste.
  11. Serve hot with tomato ketchup or your favourite dipping sauce.

Serving

Serve the crispy fries with ketchup for the best feeling although people take mayo too

Enjoy it
