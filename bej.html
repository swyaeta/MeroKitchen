Beijing Chicken

Crispy chicken tossed with vegetables and a spicy oyster sauce.

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Black dried mushrooms - 3–4 pieces
  • Capsicum - 1 medium
  • Onion - 1 medium
  • Boneless chicken - 300 g
  • Spring onion - 1/2 handful
  • Dried red chillies - 3–4 pieces
  • Garlic - 3–4 cloves
  • Salt to taste
  • White pepper powder - 1/3 tbsp
  • Cornflour - 1 tbsp
  • Refined flour - 2 tbsp
  • Hot sauce - to taste
  • Oyster sauce - 3 tbsp
  • Cooking oil - as required for deep-frying
  • Water - as required

Cooking Procedure

  1. Soak the dried mushrooms in warm water until soft. Drain them and cut them into small pieces.
  2. Cut the boneless chicken into bite-sized pieces. Slice the onion and capsicum, chop the garlic, and cut the spring onions.
  3. Put the chicken in a bowl. Add salt, white pepper powder, cornflour, and refined flour. Mix well until the chicken pieces are evenly coated. Add a little water if needed.
  4. Heat cooking oil in a deep pan. Fry the chicken in small batches until golden, crispy, and fully cooked. The centre of each piece should reach 74°C (165°F).
  5. Remove the fried chicken and let the excess oil drain.
  6. Heat a little fresh oil in a wok or frying pan. Add the chopped garlic and dried red chillies. Stir-fry briefly until fragrant.
  7. Add the onion and capsicum. Stir-fry for a few minutes, keeping the vegetables slightly crunchy.
  8. Add the softened mushrooms and stir everything together.
  9. Add the oyster sauce and hot sauce. Mix well. Add a small amount of water if the sauce needs thinning.
  10. Add the fried chicken to the pan and toss until the chicken and vegetables are coated in the sauce.
  11. Taste and add salt only if needed. Add the chopped spring onions and mix briefly.
  12. Turn off the heat and serve immediately while the chicken is hot.
