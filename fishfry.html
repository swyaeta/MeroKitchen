Fried Fish

Crispy fried fish coated with aromatic spices and a delicious golden crust.

Cooking time: 2 hrs

Back to home

Ingredients

  • 500 g fish pieces
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons rice flour
  • 1 tablespoon cornflour
  • Cooking oil for frying
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Cooking procedure:

  1. Clean the fish pieces and pat them dry.
  2. Mix lemon juice, turmeric, chili powder, cumin, coriander, ginger-garlic paste, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Coat the fish pieces with the spice mixture.
  4. Let the fish marinate for 15 to 20 minutes.
  5. Mix rice flour and cornflour in a separate bowl.
  6. Coat the marinated fish with the flour mixture.
  7. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  8. Carefully place the fish pieces into the hot oil.
  9. Fry until golden brown and cooked through, turning carefully.
  10. Remove the fish and place it on a plate lined with paper towels.

Ready to Eat?

Serve hot with lemon wedges, fresh salad, and spicy tomato achar.
