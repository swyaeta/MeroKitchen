Chicken Sekuwa

A popular Nepali grilled chicken dish marinated with traditional spices.

cooking time: 1 hr 30 min

Back to home

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken, cut into cubes
  • 2 tablespoons mustard oil
  • 2 tablespoons yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Cooking procedure:

  1. Wash the chicken and cut it into medium-sized cubes.
  2. Mix yogurt, mustard oil, ginger-garlic paste, and all the spices in a bowl.
  3. Add the chicken and coat it well with the marinade.
  4. Cover and let the chicken marinate for at least 1 hour.
  5. Thread the chicken pieces onto skewers.
  6. Grill the chicken, turning occasionally until golden brown.
  7. Cook until the chicken is fully cooked inside.
  8. Sprinkle lemon juice and fresh coriander over the chicken.

Ready to Eat?

Serve hot with beaten rice, spicy tomato achar, sliced onions, and fresh green chilies.
