Grilled Chicken

Juicy grilled chicken marinated with herbs, spices, and fresh lemon juice.

cooking time: 1 hr 15 min

Back to home

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken pieces
  • 2 tablespoons yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon paprika or chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon mixed herbs
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Cooking procedure:

  1. Wash the chicken and make small cuts in the pieces.
  2. Mix yogurt, lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste, and all the spices in a bowl.
  3. Add the chicken and coat it well with the marinade.
  4. Cover and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Preheat the grill or oven.
  6. Brush the chicken with a little oil.
  7. Grill the chicken, turning occasionally for even cooking.
  8. Cook until the chicken is fully cooked inside and golden on the outside.
  9. Let it rest for a few minutes before serving.

Ready to Eat?

Serve hot with grilled vegetables, fresh salad, lemon wedges, or your favorite sauce.
