Egg Curry

Cooking time:40 hrs

Boiled eggs covered in a spicy, flavourful masala gravy. Simple, comforting, and delicious!

Back to home

Ingredients

  • Eggs - 4 pieces
  • Onions - 2 medium pieces
  • Tomatoes - 2 medium pieces
  • Ginger-garlic paste - 1 tbsp
  • Green chilli - 1 piece (optional)
  • Cooking oil or mustard oil - 2 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds - 1/2 tsp
  • Turmeric powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Red chilli powder - 1/2 tsp or to taste
  • Coriander powder - 1 tsp
  • Cumin powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Garam masala - 1/2 tsp
  • Salt - to taste
  • Water - as required
  • Fresh coriander - for garnishing (optional)

Cooking Procedure

  1. Boil the eggs for about 10-12 minutes until fully cooked.
  2. Let them cool, peel off the shells, and make a few shallow cuts in each egg.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and lightly fry the eggs with a pinch of turmeric and salt. Remove and keep aside.
  4. In the same pan, add cumin seeds and let them sizzle.
  5. Add chopped onions and cook until golden brown.
  6. Add the ginger-garlic paste and green chilli. Cook for about a minute.
  7. Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt.
  8. Cook until the tomatoes soften and the masala becomes thick.
  9. Add water to make the gravy and let it simmer for 5-7 minutes.
  10. Add the eggs and gently coat them with the masala.
  11. Sprinkle garam masala and simmer for another 3-5 minutes.
  12. Garnish with fresh coriander if desired.
  13. Serve hot with rice, roti, or naan. Enjoy!

Serving

Serve it with rice or roti and raw onion slices

Pro tip: Make a few cuts in the boiled eggs so they soak up more of that delicious curry flavour!
