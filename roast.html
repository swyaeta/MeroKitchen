Chicken Roast

A delicious and spicy homemade chicken roast.

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces - 3 kg
  • Salt to taste
  • White pepper powder - 1 tbsp
  • Ginger-garlic paste - 1 tbsp
  • Lemon juice - 1 tbsp
  • Refined flour - 2 tbsp
  • Corn flour - 1 tbsp
  • Hot sauce - 1 tbsp
  • MSG - 1 pinch (optional)

Cooking Procedure

  1. Wash the chicken pieces and pat them dry with a clean paper towel.
  2. In a large bowl, add salt, white pepper powder, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, and hot sauce. Mix well.
  3. Add the refined flour, corn flour, and optional MSG. Mix until the chicken pieces are evenly coated.
  4. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for 2–4 hours so the chicken absorbs the flavours.
  5. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Arrange the chicken pieces in a single layer on a baking tray.
  6. Roast for approximately 40–60 minutes, depending on the size of the pieces. Turn the pieces halfway through cooking.
  7. Check that the thickest part of the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 74°C (165°F). Avoid eating chicken that is not fully cooked.
  8. Remove the chicken from the oven and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Serving

Serve hot with salad, lemon wedges, or your favourite dipping sauce.
