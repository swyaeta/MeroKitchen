Chicken Chop Suey

A delicious Chinese-style dish with chicken, fresh vegetables, and savory sauce.

cooking time: 40 min

Back to home

Ingredients

  • 300 g chicken, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup cabbage, chopped
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced
  • 1 capsicum, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cornflour
  • 1 cup chicken stock or water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Cooking procedure:

  1. Wash and prepare all the vegetables.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and cook the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the sliced chicken and cook thoroughly.
  4. Add the onion, carrot, cabbage, and capsicum.
  5. Stir-fry the vegetables for a few minutes until slightly tender.
  6. Mix the soy sauce, cornflour, and chicken stock in a bowl.
  7. Pour the sauce into the pan and stir everything together.
  8. Cook until the sauce thickens and the vegetables are tender.
  9. Add salt and pepper to taste.
  10. Serve hot with steamed rice or noodles.

Ready to Eat?

Enjoy your homemade Chicken Chop Suey with fried rice or noodles!
