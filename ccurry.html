Chicken Curry

Cooking time: 1.5 hrs

A warm, spicy, flavourful curry that tastes amazing with rice or roti.

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Chicken - 500 g
  • Onions - 2 medium pieces
  • Tomatoes - 2 medium pieces
  • Potatoes - 2 medium pieces (optional)
  • Ginger-garlic paste - 1 tbsp
  • Green chillies - 1,2 pieces
  • Mustard oil or cooking oil - 3 tbsp
  • Turmeric powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Red chilli powder - 1 tsp or to taste
  • Coriander powder - 1 tsp
  • Cumin powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Garam masala - 1/2 tsp
  • Salt - to taste
  • Water - as required
  • Fresh coriander - for garnishing (optional)

Cooking Procedure

  1. Wash the chicken and drain the excess water.
  2. Chop the onions, tomatoes, potatoes, and green chillies.
  3. Heat oil in a deep pan over medium heat.
  4. Add the chopped onions and fry until they turn golden brown.
  5. Add the ginger-garlic paste and green chillies. Cook for about a minute.
  6. Add the turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt. Mix well.
  7. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they soften and form a thick masala.
  8. Add the chicken and mix thoroughly so the masala coats every piece.
  9. Cook for about 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  10. Add the potatoes if using, along with enough water to make the curry gravy.
  11. Cover the pan and simmer for 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken and potatoes are fully cooked.
  12. Add the garam masala and mix well. Adjust the gravy with a little more water if needed.
  13. Garnish with fresh coriander if you like.
  14. Serve hot with rice, roti, or beaten rice. Enjoy!

Serving

Pro tip: Let the curry rest for a few minutes before serving so the flavours can come together.

Serve with rice or roti and slices of onions for enhanced taste
