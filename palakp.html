Palak Paneer

Cooking time: 1.5 hrs

Soft paneer in a creamy, flavourful spinach gravy. Healthy, comforting, and delicious!

Back to home

Ingredients

  • Paneer - 250 g
  • Fresh spinach (palak) - 250 g
  • Onion - 1 medium piece
  • Tomato - 1 medium piece
  • Ginger-garlic paste - 1 tbsp
  • Green chilli - 1 piece (optional)
  • Cooking oil or ghee - 2 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds - 1/2 tsp
  • Turmeric powder - 1/4 tsp
  • Red chilli powder - 1/2 tsp or to taste
  • Coriander powder - 1 tsp
  • Garam masala - 1/2 tsp
  • Salt - to taste
  • Water - as required
  • Fresh cream - 2 tbsp (optional)

Cooking Procedure

  1. Wash the spinach thoroughly to remove dirt.
  2. Boil water in a pot and add the spinach for 2 minutes.
  3. Transfer the spinach to cold water, then drain it.
  4. Blend the spinach with the green chilli into a smooth paste.
  5. Cut the paneer into cubes and chop the onion and tomato.
  6. Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle.
  7. Add the chopped onion and cook until golden brown.
  8. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for about a minute.
  9. Add the chopped tomato, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, and salt.
  10. Cook until the tomato softens and the masala becomes thick.
  11. Add the blended spinach and mix well. Simmer for 5-7 minutes.
  12. Add the paneer cubes and gently mix them into the gravy.
  13. Sprinkle garam masala and simmer for another 3-5 minutes.
  14. Add fresh cream if desired and mix gently.
  15. Serve hot with rice, roti, or naan. Enjoy!

Serving

Serve it with roti or rice and some slices of raw onions

Pro tip: Don't overcook the spinach, or it may lose its fresh green colour!
