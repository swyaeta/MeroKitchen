Soybean Ko Choila

A spicy and flavourful Nepali Newari-style soybean dish.

Cooking time: 30 min

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Dry soybeans - 2 cups
  • Mustard oil - 3 tbsp
  • Garlic - 5 cloves, finely chopped
  • Ginger - 1 tbsp, finely chopped
  • Green chillies - 2–3, chopped
  • Red chilli powder - 1 tsp (adjust to taste)
  • Turmeric powder - 1/4 tsp
  • Cumin powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Fenugreek seeds - 1/2 tsp
  • Salt - to taste
  • Fresh coriander - 2 tbsp, chopped
  • Lemon juice - 1 tbsp
  • Spring onion - 2 tbsp, chopped (optional)

Cooking Procedure

  1. Soak the soybeans in water overnight to help them soften.
  2. Drain and rinse the soybeans. Boil them in fresh water until completely tender, then drain well.
  3. Heat a little mustard oil in a pan and cook the soybeans until lightly browned. Set them aside.
  4. In a bowl, combine the cooked soybeans with garlic, ginger, green chillies, red chilli powder, turmeric, cumin powder, and salt.
  5. Heat the remaining mustard oil in a small pan. Add the fenugreek seeds and let them darken slightly.
  6. Carefully pour the hot seasoned oil over the soybean mixture and mix well.
  7. Add lemon juice, chopped coriander, and spring onion if desired. Toss everything together so the flavours combine.
  8. Let the choila rest for a few minutes before serving.

Serving

Serve soybean ko choila with beaten rice (chiura), sliced onions, fresh coriander, and your favourite Nepali side dishes.
