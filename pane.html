Paneer Pakoda

Crispy and fried paneer with alot of spices and yummy flavour

Cooking time:30 mins

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Paneer - 250g
  • Salt - acc to your taste
  • Gram flour - 2tbs
  • Refine flour - 1 tbs
  • Turmeric- 1/4 tbs
  • Cumin powder -1/2 tbs
  • Coriander powder- 1/3 tbs
  • Chilli powder - acc to your tolerence
  • Baking powder- 1 pinch
  • Chopped coriander -1/3 handful
  • Green chopped chilli - 2 pieces ( acc to your taste)
  • Onion chopped -1/2 piece
  • ajoions seeds -1/4 tbs
  • oil - for deep frying

Cooking procedure

  1. Cut the paneer into small cubes or bite-sized pieces.
  2. In a large bowl, add gram flour, refined flour, salt, turmeric powder, cumin powder, coriander powder, and chilli powder.
  3. Add baking powder and ajwain seeds (carom seeds). Mix all the dry ingredients together.
  4. Add chopped coriander, green chillies, and chopped onion to the mixture.
  5. Add a little water at a time and mix until you get a thick batter that can coat the paneer. Do not make it too watery.
  6. Add the paneer pieces and gently mix until they are evenly coated with the batter.
  7. Heat oil in a deep pan over medium heat.
  8. Carefully drop the coated paneer pieces into the hot oil in small batches. Do not overcrowd the pan.
  9. Fry until the pakodas turn golden brown and crispy, turning them occasionally for even cooking.
  10. Remove the pakodas and place them on a rack or paper towel to drain excess oil.

Serving

Serve the crispy paneer pakoda eith chaiya/chai (mainly in rainny season)

Enjoy it
