Vegetable Fried Rice

Delicious fried rice tossed with fresh vegetables and flavorful sauces.

cooking time: 30 min

Back to home

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup cabbage, chopped
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • Salt to taste

Cooking procedure:

  1. Cook the rice and let it cool before frying.
  2. Heat oil in a pan or wok.
  3. Add garlic and onion and fry until fragrant.
  4. Add the carrot, cabbage, and capsicum.
  5. Stir-fry the vegetables for a few minutes.
  6. Add the cooked rice and mix everything well.
  7. Pour in the soy sauce and add black pepper.
  8. Stir-fry for another few minutes until everything is heated through.
  9. Add chopped spring onions and mix well.
  10. Serve hot and enjoy your vegetable fried rice!

Ready to Eat?

Serve hot with vegetable manchurian, chili sauce, or your favorite curry.
