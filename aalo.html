Aalo Neemkin

Prep min: 30 min if you already have neemkin

Perfect taste of crispiness and masala all over your mouth

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Boiled potatoes - 3,4 pieces
  • Boiled chana - 1 fistful
  • Namkeen - 1 fistful
  • Bhujiya - 1 fistful
  • Green chilli - 1 piece
  • Chopped coriander - 1/2 fistful
  • Chopped onion - 1 medium piece
  • Salt to taste
  • Black salt to taste
  • Lemon - 1 piece
  • Mustard oil - 1 tbsp
  • Coriander seeds - 1/2 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds - 1/2 tbsp
  • Dried red chillies - 4,5 pieces ( acc to your taste tho)
  • Chat masala - 1 tbsp

Ingredients for Sauce

  • Gram flour or refined flour - 2 tbsp
  • Water as required
  • Salt - 1 pinch
  • Chat masala - 1/2 tbsp

Cooking Procedure

  1. Boil the potatoes and chana until they are cooked properly.
  2. Peel and chop the boiled potatoes into small pieces.
  3. Dry roast the coriander seeds, cumin seeds, and dried red chillies. Grind them into a coarse powder.
  4. In a large bowl, add the boiled potatoes, boiled chana, chopped onion, green chilli, and coriander.
  5. Add the namkeen and bhujiya.
  6. Add salt, black salt, chat masala, and the ground spice mixture.
  7. Add mustard oil and squeeze in the lemon juice.
  8. Mix everything and sauce together until the ingredients are properly combined.

Making the Sauce

  1. Take the gram flour or refined flour in a bowl.
  2. Add a pinch of salt and chat masala.
  3. Gradually add water and mix until you get the required consistency.
  4. Prepare the sauce according to your preferred thickness.

Serve your Aalo Neemkin and eat a big bowlll!!!!
