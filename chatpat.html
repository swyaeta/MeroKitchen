Flavourful mix of everything

Ingredients

  • Onions - 1,2 pieces
  • Boiled potatoes - 1,2 pieces
  • Green chillies - 1,2 pieces (according to your tolerance, btw)
  • Puffed rice - 100 g
  • Instant noodles (Wai Wai is the best)
  • Tomato - 1 piece
  • Peanuts (optional)
  • Salt, ofc
  • Roasted cumin powder
  • Your favourite sauce
  • Lemon juice - optional
  • Fresh coriander - optional

Preparation Procedure

  1. Boil the potatoes until they are soft. Let them cool, peel them, and cut them into small cubes.
  2. Finely chop the onions, tomatoes, green chillies, and fresh coriander if you're using it.
  3. Take a large mixing bowl and add the chopped onions, tomatoes, boiled potatoes, and green chillies.
  4. Break the Wai Wai noodles into small pieces and add them to the bowl.
  5. Add the puffed rice and optional peanuts.
  6. Add the Wai Wai seasoning, salt to taste, roasted cumin powder, and your favourite sauce.
  7. If you like it extra tangy, squeeze in some lemon juice.
  8. Mix everything thoroughly until the ingredients are evenly combined and the mixture becomes nice and flavourful.
  9. Taste it and adjust the salt, spices, and sauce according to your preference.
  10. Serve immediately before the puffed rice and noodles lose their crunch.

Serving

Eat it fat otherwise it won't be that good, share it with your friends, or keep the whole bowl to yourself. No judgement here.
