Kheer

A creamy and delicious traditional Nepali rice pudding.

Cooking time: 50 mins

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Full-fat milk - 1 litre
  • Rice - 1/4 cup
  • Sugar - 1/3 cup (adjust to taste)
  • Ghee - 1 tsp
  • Cardamom powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Cashew nuts - 2 tbsp (optional)
  • Raisins - 2 tbsp (optional)
  • Almonds - 2 tbsp, chopped (optional)
  • Saffron - a few strands (optional)

Cooking Procedure

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly under running water and drain it.
  2. Heat the milk in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat, stirring occasionally to prevent it from sticking.
  3. When the milk starts to boil, add the washed rice and reduce the heat to low.
  4. Cook for 30-35 minutes, stirring frequently, until the rice becomes soft and the milk thickens.
  5. Add the sugar and ghee, then stir until the sugar dissolves completely.
  6. Add cardamom powder, chopped nuts, raisins, and saffron if desired.
  7. Cook for another 5 minutes, stirring gently.
  8. Turn off the heat and allow the kheer to cool slightly. It will thicken further as it cools.

Serving

Serve kheer warm or chilled, topped with chopped nuts. Enjoy this traditional dessert during festivals and family gatherings.
