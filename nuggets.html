Chicken Nuggets

Crispy on the outside and juicy on the inside.

Cooking time: 1.5 hrs

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken - 500 g
  • Salt - to taste
  • Black pepper powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Garlic paste - 1 tsp
  • Ginger paste - 1/2 tsp
  • White pepper powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Cornflour - 2 tbsp
  • Refined flour - 3 tbsp
  • Egg - 1
  • Breadcrumbs - 1 cup
  • Water or milk - 2 tbsp, if needed
  • Cooking oil - as required for frying

Cooking Procedure

  1. Cut the boneless chicken into small pieces and grind or mince it until finely chopped.
  2. Put the chicken in a bowl. Add salt, black pepper, white pepper, ginger paste, and garlic paste.
  3. Add 1 tablespoon of cornflour and 1 tablespoon of refined flour. Mix everything until well combined.
  4. Shape the mixture into small, flat nugget pieces using clean hands.
  5. Prepare three bowls. Put the remaining refined flour and cornflour in the first bowl, beat the egg in the second bowl, and place the breadcrumbs in the third.
  6. Coat each nugget lightly in the flour mixture, dip it into the beaten egg, and then cover it with breadcrumbs.
  7. Place the coated nuggets in the refrigerator for 15–20 minutes to help them hold their shape.
  8. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Carefully add the nuggets in small batches.
  9. Fry until golden brown, turning them as needed. Make sure the chicken is completely cooked and reaches an internal temperature of 74°C (165°F).
  10. Remove the nuggets and place them on a rack or paper towel to drain excess oil.

Serving

Serve crispy!!!! with tomato ketchup, mayonnaise, or your favourite dipping sauce.

Enjoy it
