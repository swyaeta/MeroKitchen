Cauli Curry

A simple, delicious homemade cauliflower curry!

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower
  • 2 medium potatoes (optional)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (or to taste)
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 cup water (as needed)
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Cooking Procedure

  1. Wash the cauliflower and cut it into small florets. Peel and chop the potatoes if using them.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add the chopped onion. Cook until it becomes soft and lightly golden.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for about a minute.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, turmeric, cumin, coriander powder, chilli powder, and salt.
  5. Cook until the tomatoes soften and the spices are well mixed.
  6. Add the cauliflower and potatoes. Mix everything well so the vegetables are coated with the masala.
  7. Add a little water, cover the pan, and cook on medium-low heat until the vegetables are tender. Stir occasionally.
  8. Check the salt and make sure the cauliflower and potatoes are fully cooked.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with rice or roti!
