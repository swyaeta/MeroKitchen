Rajma Curry

A comforting Indian kidney bean curry cooked in a rich tomato gravy with aromatic spices.

cooking time: 1 hr 30 min

Back to home

Ingredients

  • 2 cups kidney beans (rajma), soaked overnight
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish
  • Water as needed

Cooking procedure:

  1. Soak the kidney beans in water overnight.
  2. Drain the water and rinse the beans thoroughly.
  3. Pressure-cook the beans in fresh water until completely soft.
  4. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  5. Add chopped onions and cook until golden brown.
  6. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  7. Add tomato puree, turmeric, chili powder, and coriander powder.
  8. Cook until the tomato mixture thickens and the oil begins to separate.
  9. Add the cooked kidney beans and mix well.
  10. Add water as needed and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes.
  11. Add garam masala and salt to taste.
  12. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.

Ready to Eat?

Serve hot with steamed rice, jeera rice, or fresh roti for a delicious meal.
