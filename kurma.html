Kurma

Crispy, crunchy, and delicious traditional Nepali sweet snacks.

Back to Home

Cooking time: 45 min

Ingredients

  • Refined flour - 2 cups
  • Ghee - 3 tbsp
  • Sugar - 1/2 cup
  • Water - as required
  • Cardamom powder - 1/2 tsp, optional
  • Cooking oil - as required for frying

Cooking Procedure

  1. Put the refined flour in a large bowl and add the ghee.
  2. Mix the flour and ghee until the mixture becomes crumbly.
  3. Gradually add water and knead into a firm, smooth dough.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rest for about 15–20 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into small portions and roll each portion into a long, thin strip.
  6. Cut the strips into small pieces and shape them as desired.
  7. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium-low heat.
  8. Fry the pieces in small batches until they become crisp and golden brown.
  9. Remove the fried pieces and place them on a paper towel to drain excess oil.
  10. In another pan, combine the sugar with a little water and heat until it forms a light, sticky syrup.
  11. Add the fried kurma pieces and mix gently until they are coated with the syrup.
  12. Spread the pieces on a plate and allow them to cool completely until the sugar coating sets.

Serving

Serve the crunchy kurma as a sweet snack with tea or during festivals and celebrations.

Enjoy your homemade Kurma!
