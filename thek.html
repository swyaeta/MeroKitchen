Thekuwa

A traditional homemade Nepali sweet, crispy on the outside and rich in flavour.

Cooking time: 30 mins

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • Wheat flour - 2 cups
  • Jaggery - 1/2 cup
  • Ghee - 1/4 cup
  • Cardamom powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Grated coconut - 2 tbsp (optional)
  • Sesame seeds - 2 tbsp (optional)
  • Fennel seeds - 1 tsp (optional)
  • Water - as needed
  • Oil or ghee for frying

Cooking Procedure

  1. Heat a little water in a pan and add the jaggery. Stir until it dissolves, then allow it to cool.
  2. Put the wheat flour in a large bowl and add the ghee. Mix well until the flour becomes crumbly.
  3. Add cardamom powder, grated coconut, sesame seeds, and fennel seeds if desired.
  4. Gradually add the cooled jaggery water and mix to make a firm dough. Add only enough water to bring the dough together.
  5. Take small portions of dough and shape them into flat rounds or traditional decorative shapes.
  6. Heat oil in a deep pan over low to medium heat.
  7. Carefully add the shaped dough and fry slowly until golden brown and cooked through.
  8. Remove the Thekuwa and place it on a plate lined with paper towels to drain excess oil.
  9. Allow it to cool completely. Thekuwa becomes crispier as it cools.

Serving

Serve Thekuwa with tea or enjoy it as a traditional Nepali sweet during festivals and family gatherings.
