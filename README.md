## Cooking recipe website:

#### Welcome to Mero Kitchen!
 ####   Explore the rich and delicious flavors of Nepal with our collection of 10 starters, 6 curries, 8 main dishes, and 6 desserts. #### This website is created to make discovering and preparing famous Nepali dishes easier, faster, and more enjoyable.
 #### From traditional snacks and flavorful curries to hearty meals and sweet homemade desserts, you can explore a variety of recipes with simple ingredients and step-by-step instructions. Whether you are a beginner learning to cook or someone who loves homemade food
  #### Mero Kitchen is here to bring the taste of Nepal to your kitchen! 


### Demo Link:
https://swyaeta.github.io/MeroKitchen/
## Features

#### Collection of starters, curries, main dishes, and desserts
#### Individual pages for different recipes
#### Ingredients listed with quantities
#### Step-by-step cooking procedures
#### Simple navigation between the homepage and recipe pages
#### Information about preparation and cooking times

## How to Run Locally:
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the project folder in Visual Studio Code.
3. Open `index.html` directly in a web browser, or use the Live Server extension.
4. Browse the homepage and open individual recipe pages.
