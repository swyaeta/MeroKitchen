Matar Paneer

1 hour

Soft paneer and green peas cooked in a rich, creamy, flavourful masala gravy.

Back to home

Ingredients

  • Paneer - 250 g
  • Green peas - 1 cup
  • Onions - 2 medium pieces
  • Tomatoes - 2 medium pieces
  • Ginger-garlic paste - 1 tbsp
  • Green chilli - 1 piece (optional)
  • Cooking oil or ghee - 2 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds - 1/2 tsp
  • Turmeric powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Red chilli powder - 1/2 tsp or to taste
  • Coriander powder - 1 tsp
  • Cumin powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Garam masala - 1/2 tsp
  • Salt - to taste
  • Water - as required
  • Fresh coriander - for garnishing (optional)
  • Fresh cream - 2 tbsp (optional)

Cooking Procedure

  1. Cut the paneer into medium-sized cubes and chop the onions and tomatoes.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle.
  3. Add the chopped onions and cook until golden brown.
  4. Add the ginger-garlic paste and green chilli. Cook for another minute.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt.
  6. Cook until the tomatoes soften and the masala becomes thick.
  7. Let the masala cool slightly, then blend it into a smooth paste if you prefer a creamy gravy.
  8. Return the masala to the pan and add the green peas with some water.
  9. Cover and cook until the peas become tender.
  10. Add the paneer cubes and gently mix them into the gravy.
  11. Simmer for 5-7 minutes, then add garam masala.
  12. Add fresh cream if desired and mix gently.
  13. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with rice, roti, or naan.

Serving

Pro tip: Don't stir too roughly after adding paneer, or the cubes may break apart!

Eat it with rice or roti and raw onions for enhanced flavour
