Chicken Biryani

Aromatic rice, rich spices, and homemade goodness.

Cooking time: 2 hrs

Back to home

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 g chicken
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tomatoes
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Biryani masala and other spices
  • Fresh mint and coriander
  • Cooking oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste

Cooking procedure:

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, spices, and ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Fry the onions until golden brown.
  4. Cook the chicken with tomatoes and spices.
  5. Boil the rice until it is partially cooked.
  6. Layer the rice and chicken in a pot with mint and coriander.
  7. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy and the chicken is fully cooked.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy your homemade biryani!

Ready to Eat?

Serve with raita, salad, or your favorite side dish.
